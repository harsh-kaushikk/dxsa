OUR VISION

To unite Sikh communities worldwide through football, elevate talent from grassroots to the global stage, and proudly represent a nation’s identity with excellence, dignity, and lasting legacy.

WHO WE ARE

The Khalistan Football Association is the governing body for Sikh football — building pathways for players, clubs, and communities to compete with pride and purpose on the world stage.

OUR IDENTITY

We represent a nation with rich history, culture, and an unbroken spirit — expressed through the beautiful game.

OUR PURPOSE

To unite our community through football, create opportunity, and inspire the next generation of athletes.

OUR PROMISE

To represent a global community with excellence — from local pitches to international competition.
